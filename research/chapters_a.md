## Welkom (front matter)
There is little subject matter here. The cover photo shows Quito, Ecuador: 20 km from the equator, at 2850 m altitude, in the Andes, on the slopes of the Pichincha volcano. There is also an example page about Shenzhen and the Parelrivierdelta. The concepts are general geography terms (evenaar, hoogteligging, megastad).

## H1 Wereldhandel in beweging
Sections: 1.1 Kantelt het economisch wereldbeeld?, 1.2 Van kolonialisme tot nu, 1.3 Verschillen tussen rollen, the country comparison VS en Zuid-Afrika, 1.4 Rol van Europa, and 1.5 Rol van Nederland.

Places that work well on a map:
- **Chokepoints:** Suezkanaal (Ever Given, 2021) and the Rode Zee (Houthi attacks, 2024).
- **Nederland:** the Shanghai to Rotterdam route (27 days); Rotterdam as the largest port in Europe; Schiphol and Rotterdam as mainports; brainport Eindhoven with ASML in Veldhoven; the Aalsmeer flower auction supplied from Kenia.
- **Production chains:** cotton from Kazachstan, spun in Turkije, woven in India, sewn in Bangladesh.
- **China:** the Nieuwe Zijderoute (with a Chinese-funded stadium in Ndola, Zambia), the Zuid-Chinese Zee, and the Oeigoeren in Xinjiang.
- **Colonial history:** Potosí silver, Niger as a former French colony, and DR Congo as an exploitatiekolonie compared with the VS as a vestigingskolonie.
- **Gini-coëfficiënt:** Brazilië 0.52, VS 0.41, NL 0.26, Slowakije 0.24.
- **Blocs and players:** BRICS members; Qatar and Saudi-Arabië investing in sport.
- **Europa:** the Ruhrgebied as an example of de-industrialisatie, and car factories in Tsjechië.
- **Zuid-Afrika:** its three capitals.

## H2 Schatkist aarde?
Sections: Vaardigheden (dwarsdoorsnede Venlo-Geleen, gesteenten determineren), 2.1 Geologische tijdschaal, 2.2 Gesteenten en ouderdom, 2.3 Delfstoffen, the country comparison Suriname en Nigeria, 2.4 Delfstoffen in Europa, and 2.5 Delfstoffen in Nederland.

Places that work well on a map:
- **Lithium:** the lithiumdriehoek (Jujuy, Salinas Grandes) and the protest slogan "No al litio, sí al agua".
- **Earth history:** the Yucatán cenotes (meteorite impact 66 million years ago), Pulau Weh, basalt columns in Duitsland, and the Tafelberg.
- **Mining abroad:** uranium in Niger; copper and cobalt in DR Congo.
- **Gold and mercury:** Suriname (Rosebel mine, the Wayana people on the Lawa river) and Osun in Nigeria.
- **Europa:** brown coal in Duitsland, hard coal in Polen, iron ore in Zweden (Kiruna), oil and gas in the Noordzee, nuclear power in Frankrijk, and the Russian gas dependency.
- **Nederland:** Krijt limestone in Zuid-Limburg, coal mining ending in 1974, and the Groningen gas field (Carboon source rock, Perm sandstone reservoir, salt seal) with its earthquakes.

## H3 Migratie
Sections: Vaardigheden (stappenplan meningsvorming, waardenkwadrant), 3.1 Migratie in de wereld, 3.2 Herkomstgebieden, 3.3 Bestemmingsgebieden, the country comparison VAE en Mexico, 3.4 Migratie en de EU, and 3.5 Migratie en Nederland.

Places that work well on a map:
- **Migration corridors:** the top corridors (Mexico to VS, Syrië to Turkije, India to VAE) and the Mexico-VS border (Rio Grande, Ciudad Juárez/El Paso).
- **Gulf states:** Dubai, where about 80% of the population are migrant workers, and Abu Dhabi.
- **Refugees:** the top countries of origin (Afghanistan, Syrië, Venezuela, Oekraïne, Zuid-Soedan) and the top host countries (Iran, Turkije, Colombia, Duitsland, Pakistan).
- **Remittances (geldzendingen):** India and Ghana as receivers.
- **Segregatie:** Manila, New York and Den Haag.
- **EU outer border:** Canarische Eilanden, Ceuta and Kroatië, plus asylum requests per country.
- **Nederland:** colonial migrants (Suriname, Antillen, Molukken), guest workers (Spanje, Italië, Turkije, Marokko), and Polen as the largest group of EU migrants.
