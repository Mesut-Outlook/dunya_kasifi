## H5 Gewapende conflicten
Sections: Vaardigheden (schaalniveaus and dimensies), 5.1 Wapengeweld wereldwijd, 5.2 Oorzaken, 5.3 Gevolgen, the country comparison Mexico en Niger, 5.4 Hoe rustig is Europa?, and 5.5 Nederland: oorlog en vrede.

Places that work well on a map:
- **Contested areas:** Kasjmir, Taiwan, the Palestijnse gebieden, Krim and Georgië.
- **Genocides:** Rwanda 1994, Cambodja 1975-79, Srebrenica 1995, and the Rohingya from Myanmar.
- **Separatism and autonomy:** Catalonië, Baskenland, Noord-Ierland, Baluchistan and Nigeria (Biafra).
- **Sahel:** Mali, Niger (Niamey coup 2023) and Sudan (Darfur).
- **Mexico:** Chiapas and the drug cartels.
- **Nederland:** the Internationaal Strafhof in Den Haag, Indonesië (1949), and missions such as Mali, Hormuz and the Golf van Aden.

## Content notes
- The conflict facts are neutral and descriptive, suitable for age 14. There is no graphic detail.
- For contested places (Palestijnse gebieden, Taiwan, Kasjmir, Krim), the facts repeat the book's framing, including who does or does not recognise them. The `country` code is a practical choice for placing the marker, not a statement about sovereignty. Kasjmir uses IN, Krim uses UA and Taiwan uses TW.
